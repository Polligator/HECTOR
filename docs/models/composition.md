# Training composition

What each model was trained on. Each patch of the circle is one cell type, and
its area is that cell type's share of the training cells; colour groups the cell
types by broad Cell Ontology term. Point at a patch for its name and how many of
its cells went into training. Many cell types are small, so open the full page to
zoom in on them.
